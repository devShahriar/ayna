Features

Four things that decide whether a question gets answered or turns into another argument about dashboards.

Ask in plain language

Type the question the way you would ask a colleague. Meridian writes the query, runs it, and shows you the SQL it used so nothing is a black box.

Warehouse native

Connects directly to Postgres, Snowflake, BigQuery and DuckDB. No extraction step, no nightly sync to babysit, no second copy of your data to secure.

Metrics that agree

Define a metric once and every chart, alert and export uses that definition. When someone changes it, everyone downstream sees what changed and why.

Alerts worth reading

Set a threshold, get a message when it breaks — with the context that explains it, not just a number turning red at 3am.

FAQ

The three questions data teams ask us before they connect anything.

Does my data leave my warehouse?

No. Meridian issues queries against your warehouse and holds results only as long as it takes to render them.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are querying within twenty minutes of connecting a warehouse.

Can I keep using dbt?

Yes. Meridian reads your dbt models and can import metric definitions directly.
